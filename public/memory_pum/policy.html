Privacy Policy for MEMORY PUM!

Last updated: 2025-04-06

Introduction
Welcome to MEMORY PUM!. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information collected while you use our game.
Information We Collect
MEMORY PUM! is a simple memory game that only stores the following non-sensitive information: • Nickname: When you achieve a high score, you can enter a nickname to appear on the leaderboard. • Game Statistics: Your game scores and achievements. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.
How We Use Information
The nickname and game statistics are used solely for: • Displaying scores on the local leaderboard • Tracking your game progress and achievements • Improving your gaming experience
Data Storage
All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not: • Collect personal information • Use cookies • Share any information with third parties • Track user behavior • Use analytics services
Children's Privacy
MEMORY PUM! is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from children or adults.
Your Rights
You have the right to: • Clear all game data through the settings menu • Use the app without providing any personal information • Use a pseudonym instead of your real name for the leaderboard
Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
