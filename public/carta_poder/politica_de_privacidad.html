Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 31, 2025

Welcome to the Power of Attorney Creator app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use our application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

When you use our application to create power of attorney documents, you may provide us with the following types of information:

  • Personal details such as names, addresses, and identification numbers of the grantor, grantee, and witnesses
  • Details about the purpose and scope of the power of attorney
  • Dates related to the document (issuance date, expiration date)
  • Other information you choose to include in your power of attorney document

Important: We do not store the information you enter to generate power of attorney documents on our servers. This data is only temporarily processed and transmitted to the Deepseek API for document generation purposes.

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use our app, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics and interaction data
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • General location information (country or region)

This information helps us improve our app's performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Document Generation: To process your inputs through the Deepseek API and generate the requested power of attorney documents.
  • App Functionality: To provide and maintain our service, including detecting and addressing technical issues.
  • Subscription Management: To process payments, manage your subscription, and provide access to premium features.
  • App Improvement: To analyze how our app is used, identify areas for improvement, and develop new features.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide assistance.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with the following parties:

  • Deepseek API: The information you enter for document generation is processed by the Deepseek API to create your power of attorney documents.
  • Service Providers: We work with third-party service providers who help us operate our business and provide services (e.g., payment processors, analytics providers).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Since we do not store the information you input for document generation, this data is not at risk of being compromised from our servers.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information

  • The right to data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section.
