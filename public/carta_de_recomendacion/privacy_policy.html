Privacy Policy for Recommendation Letter

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Recommendation Letter mobile app (the "App") collects, uses and shares information. It applies to the Android and iOS versions.

The App has no user accounts. We never ask you to register, and we hold no profile about you.

2. The letters you write

To draft a letter, the App sends the details you type into the form to our server, which passes them to an artificial-intelligence provider that writes the text. These details may include:

  • Names of the person recommending and the person recommended
  • Email address and phone number, if you fill them in
  • Company or organization, job title, department and relationship
  • Dates, reason for leaving, performance or payment history
  • Purpose of the letter, strengths, achievements and free-text comments

This information leaves your device. It has to: the letter is written by an AI model that runs on a server, not on your phone. It is used only to produce your letter. We do not store it and we do not write it to any log. Once the letter comes back, our server keeps nothing.

The AI provider is DeepSeek, and the request travels through Google Cloud Functions. This means your form data may be processed on servers outside your country. If you would rather not send a particular detail, simply leave that field out — only the names are required.

The finished letters are saved on your device, so you can find them later in "My letters". They stay there until you delete them in the App or uninstall it. They are not uploaded anywhere and we cannot see them.

4. Advertising and your choices

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads may use your advertising ID and approximate location.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before it requests any ad. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad preferences inside the App.
  • On iOS, the App asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • A premium subscription removes ads entirely.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card details. RevenueCat only tells the App whether your subscription is active.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Your letters: stored on your device only. Delete them in the App, or uninstall it, and they are gone. We hold no copy.
  • Form details: not retained by our server after the letter is generated.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Google under Firebase's retention settings.

Because there are no accounts, there is nothing on our side tied to your identity to hand over or erase. If you have a question about your data, write to us at the address below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. In practice, for this App that means:

  • Deleting letters in the App, or uninstalling it, removes everything held about you on the device.
  • Ad consent can be withdrawn at any time from Settings → Ad preferences (where applicable) or through your device's tracking settings.
  • You can stop sending form data by not using the letter generator; the App's templates work without it.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date at the top of this page tells you when it was last revised.
