Privacy Policy for Recommendation Letter

Effective Date: October 11, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Recommendation Letter ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Optional Personal Information

Our App allows you to enter optional personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Company or organization name
  • Job title
  • Dates of employment or collaboration
  • Other details used to generate a recommendation letter

This information is only used locally to create your letter and is not stored, transmitted, or shared with us or third parties.

2.2 Usage and Technical Data

We may collect limited technical data to improve performance and user experience:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage data and feature interactions
  • Crash logs or diagnostic data (if enabled on your device)

This information is collected anonymously and does not identify you personally.

2.3 Purchases and Subscriptions

If you access premium features, purchases are processed through RevenueCat and the app stores (Google Play or App Store). We do not collect or store any payment details; all payment data is handled securely by these third-party services.

3. How We Use the Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Generate recommendation letters based on the data you provide
  • Improve the App’s performance and usability
  • Display relevant ads through AdMob
  • Manage purchases and subscriptions
  • Ensure legal and technical compliance

We do not use your personal information for targeted tracking beyond what is required by our advertising partners.

All third-party partners:

5. Data Retention

We do not store the information you enter into the App. Any personal information is processed locally on your device and deleted automatically when you close or reset the App.

6. Data Security

We take privacy seriously and ensure:

  • No data entered by you leaves your device
  • Secure communication for purchase processing and ads through trusted services (RevenueCat, AdMob, Google Play, App Store)

However, remember that no system is 100% secure, and you use the App at your own discretion.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal data, most data subject rights (access, correction, deletion) are not applicable. If you have any concerns regarding your data or purchases, you can contact us directly.

The App is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect any data from minors.
